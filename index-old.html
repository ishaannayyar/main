About Me

I left India a decade ago — and never stopped navigating borders.

Since I left home, I've been juggling savings in multiple currencies, watching a tech career compound across continents, and answering the same question every visa renewal: "where exactly is home?"

Along the way I learned that most professional advice is built for people who stay still. Single country. Single currency. Single career arc. International life doesn't work that way — and the gap in good education for people like us is enormous.

So I built my own frameworks for the three areas that matter most: how to save and invest when your money lives across borders, how to design a career that travels with you, and how to navigate the visa, residency and identity questions that come with all of it.

This is education, not advice. I won't tell you what to do. I'll teach you how to think — so you can ask better questions of the licensed advisors who eventually do.
